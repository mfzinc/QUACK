
QUACK

QUACK is a cozy, fast-paced platformer currently in active development, built with the Godot Engine.
What started as a fun experiment to master momentum and sliding mechanics has grown into a charming little movement-focused game. Inspired by the iconic bunny-hopping (BHOP) mechanics of Krunker, QUACK challenges you to chain your jumps and slides together to keep your speed up while soaking in a relaxing, cozy atmosphere.
Whether you're here to casually waddle through the levels or test your speedrunning skills, jump in and give it a try!

Controls
• Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
• Jump: Spacebar, W, or Up Arrow
• Slide: S or Down Arrow (Tip: Time your slides right after a jump to keep your momentum going!)

Development Status
• Engine: Godot
• Status: In Development — Feedback, bug reports, and high scores in the comments are always welcome!
